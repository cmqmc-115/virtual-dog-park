Dog Encyclopedia

Three popular breeds. Each card shows size, personality, and everyday care.

Beagle

  • Size: Small. About 33-41 cm tall, 9-11 kg.
  • Personality: Friendly, curious, and good with kids.
  • Everyday care: Needs daily walks and loves to sniff. Keep it on a leash outside.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large. About 51-61 cm tall, 25-34 kg.
  • Personality: Gentle, smart, and eager to please.
  • Everyday care: Brush often. Loves exercise and playing fetch.

Poodle

  • Size: Varies. Standard is ~38-60 cm tall, 20-32 kg.
  • Personality: Clever, alert, and active.
  • Everyday care: Needs regular grooming. Learns tricks quickly.
